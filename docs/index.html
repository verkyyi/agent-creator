How it works

Say it. Check it. Send it.

About two minutes from your truck. You approve every helper before anyone can open it.

1

Say what you need

“Brief Bay Roofing’s crew for 42 Oak St. Keep it until the job closes Friday.”

Talk or type. Crewbrain asks only for what it’s missing, like who the crew is or what time Friday.

2

Check the card

Send the link

Text it or show the QR code on site. Only the people you named can open it. A forwarded link doesn’t work for anyone else.

Questions it can’t answer come to you. Your answer becomes part of what your company knows, once you approve it.

Built for the field

Made for gloves, ladders and bad signal.

Talk, don’t type

Owners set up helpers by voice between jobs. Crews ask questions out loud with their hands full.

“Where do we park?” → “Street parking on Elm. Driveway stays clear for the homeowner.”

Their language

Write your instructions once in English. Your crew can ask and hear answers in Spanish, or any language they’re comfortable in.

“¿Cuál es el código de la puerta?” → “Es 4417, luego la tecla de numeral.”

Snap it to teach it

Photograph a spec sheet, warranty card or permit. Crewbrain reads it, and you approve it before any helper can use it.

Panel warranty card → “Warranty: Rev 3”, ready for Juno

You stay the expert

When a helper doesn’t know, it says so and sends the question to you. It never makes up a price, a date or a promise.

Inbox: “Can we add a second battery later?”, asked by Raj Patel

Access that ends

When the job closes, so does the helper.

Gate codes, lockbox numbers and site notes shouldn’t outlive the job. Every helper has an exact end time, and you can shut it off with one tap.

DraftYou describe it. Nobody can open it.
ApprovedYou checked exactly who, what and until when.
LiveNamed people only. Changes need your OK.
ClosedThe link stops working, open chats end, and job-only knowledge locks back to Office only.
A link alone isn’t a key.

Each person signs in with their phone number. A forwarded link shows nothing.

Internal stays internal.

Pricing, margins and other jobs never reach a helper. They aren’t hidden from the AI, because they’re never handed to it.

Nothing learned behind your back.

Conversations don’t become company knowledge unless you approve the answer.
