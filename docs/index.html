How it works

  1. Say it, approve it

    Describe the helper you need out loud, then approve exactly what it may see.

  2. Send it

    Text a link or show a QR code. No app, no account for the homeowner or crew.

  3. They ask, any language

    They ask by voice or text, in their own language. When the job closes, access ends.

Locked by default

Every piece of knowledge has a lock: Office only, Crew, or Homeowners. Helpers search as their people, so locked notes never come back. Built on HydraDB, Claude and OpenAI realtime voice.
